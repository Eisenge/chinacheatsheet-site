Our Mission

Help the World
Discover China

China has over 5,000 years of history, 56 ethnic groups, dramatic landscapes that stretch from Himalayan peaks to tropical coastlines, and a culinary tradition that is arguably the greatest on Earth. Yet most of the world has barely glimpsed it. Our mission is simple: to introduce China's extraordinary beauty and culture to as many people as possible — authentically, thoughtfully, and unforgettably.

How We Started

A Traveler's
Passion Became
a Purpose

China Cheat Sheet was founded by a passionate Chinese traveler who has spent years exploring every corner of this remarkable country — from the imperial hutongs of Beijing to the minority villages of Yunnan, from the desert dunes of Dunhuang to the rice terraces of Longji.

After six years working in SEO and digital marketing, our founder made a bold decision: to leave the corporate world and channel all of that expertise into something more meaningful — building a bridge between the world and China's breathtaking landscapes, ancient history, and living culture.

The name China Cheat Sheet says it all. We are your insider guide, your shortcut to the best of China — the places, experiences, and stories that most tourists never find.

Based in Shenzhen, we combine deep local knowledge with international marketing expertise to design journeys that are genuinely different from anything you'll find on a mainstream travel platform.

"I have watched foreign visitors arrive in China and leave having seen only a fraction of what this country has to offer. That always stayed with me. China is so much more than the Great Wall and the Forbidden City — and I wanted to show the world what lies beyond."

— Founder, China Cheat Sheet · Shenzhen, China
