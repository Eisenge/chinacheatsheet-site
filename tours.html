Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Do I need a visa to visit China?

Most nationalities require a Chinese tourist visa (L visa). We provide full visa guidance with every booking. As of 2025, many countries benefit from China's expanded visa-free policy — contact us to check.

What is the best time to visit China?

Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal for most destinations. Tibet is best June–September. We advise on the optimal season based on your itinerary.

Are tours private or group?

Both. All tours can run as fully private departures with a dedicated guide. We also offer small group tours (max 8–12 people) on fixed departure dates.

What physical fitness is required?

Difficulty varies by tour. Classic tours require moderate walking. Tibet involves altitudes above 5,000m and requires acclimatization days.

What's included in the price?

All tours include 4–5 star hotels, internal transport, English-speaking expert guides, all entrance fees, and 24/7 support. International flights are not included.

Can I customize any itinerary?

Absolutely — every listed tour is a starting point. Add destinations, extend nights, upgrade hotels, or combine itineraries. We tailor everything to your preferences.

What is the cancellation policy?

A 30% deposit secures your booking; balance due 60 days before departure. Cancellations more than 60 days before receive a full refund.

Do your guides speak English?

Yes — all guides are fluent English speakers. French, German, Spanish and Japanese guides also available on request.
