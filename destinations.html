Before You Go

Essential Travel Tips
for China

Visas & Entry

Most nationalities require a Chinese tourist visa (L visa), applied for at your nearest Chinese embassy. As of 2025, China has expanded visa-free access for many countries — we check current rules for your passport as part of every booking.

Internet & Apps

Google, WhatsApp, and most Western apps are blocked in China. We recommend getting a VPN before you arrive. WeChat is essential for daily life. We provide a full tech setup guide with every booking.

Money & Payments

China is increasingly cashless — WeChat Pay and Alipay dominate. International visitors can now link foreign cards to Alipay. We recommend carrying some RMB cash as backup for smaller vendors and rural areas.

Getting Around

China's high-speed rail network is world-class — fast, punctual, and affordable. Beijing to Shanghai in 4.5 hours. Domestic flights connect more remote destinations. We book all transport as part of your itinerary.

🗣

Language

Mandarin Chinese is the official language. English is spoken in major tourist areas and international hotels. Our English-speaking local guides handle all communication — you'll never feel lost or misunderstood.

🏥

Health & Safety

China is generally very safe for tourists. Travel insurance is essential — we strongly recommend coverage including medical evacuation. High-altitude destinations like Tibet require acclimatization. We provide detailed health guidance per destination.
